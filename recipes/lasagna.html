Lasagna



Description

-->

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta, rich meat sauce, creamy béchamel, and melted cheese. It's hearty, flavorful, and perfect for family dinners.

This recipe gives you a comforting, oven-baked meal that combines savory flavors with a cheesy, golden topping.


Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 500g (1 lb) ground beef or Italian sausage
  • 1 can (800g / 28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon dried basil
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 2 cups ricotta cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 3 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • Fresh basil leaves (optional, for garnish)

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Boil the lasagna noodles until al dente, then drain and set aside.
  3. Heat olive oil in a pan, sauté onion and garlic until fragrant.
  4. Add ground beef/sausage and cook until browned.
  5. Stir in crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, oregano, basil, salt, and pepper. Let it simmer for 15–20 minutes.
  6. In a bowl, mix ricotta cheese with an egg and a little Parmesan.
  7. Grease a baking dish and spread a thin layer of meat sauce at the bottom.
  8. Layer noodles, ricotta mixture, meat sauce, and mozzarella cheese. Repeat layers until dish is full.
  9. Top with mozzarella and Parmesan cheese.
  10. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes, then uncover and bake another 20 minutes until golden and bubbly.
  11. Let it rest for 10 minutes before slicing and serving.
