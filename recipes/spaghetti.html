Spaghetti



Description

Spaghetti is a traditional Italian pasta dish, usually served with a rich tomato sauce, garlic, herbs, and sometimes meat. It’s a quick, comforting meal perfect for any day of the week.

This recipe brings together tender pasta and a savory sauce that everyone will enjoy.


Ingredients

  • 400g (14 oz) spaghetti
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 can (400g / 14 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1 teaspoon dried basil
  • 1 teaspoon oregano
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Grated Parmesan cheese (for topping)
  • Fresh parsley (optional)

Steps

  1. Boil spaghetti noodles in salted water until al dente. Drain and set aside.
  2. Heat olive oil in a pan, sauté onion and garlic until fragrant.
  3. Add crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, basil, oregano, salt, and pepper. Simmer for 15 minutes.
  4. Mix cooked spaghetti into the sauce and toss well.
  5. Serve hot, topped with Parmesan cheese and parsley.
